AltF8 emulator

Melody + chord finder and a small synth with a mod matrix. Click the page, then hold F8 (Enter) to power on. Hold Play + F8 (Space + Enter) to turn off. Hold Play to open the screen fan, turn the main knob to pick a screen, let go to open it.

Switch screen: hold Play to open the fan from the top-right corner, turn the knob (the picked screen turns yellow), let go to open ithold Space, ←→, release
  • OSC: knobs are unison (1-7 voices), detune (spread between unison voices, in cents), fine tune (±100 cents) and octave. Main knob picks the wave.
  • FILTER: buttons 1/2 (or the main knob) pick filter 1 or 2. Knobs: cutoff, resonance, type (LP, HP, BP, notch) and routing (serial, parallel, filter 1 only). Yellow line = what you hear.
  • MOD: button 1 = LFO (press again for LFO 2), 2 = envelope 2, 3 = sequencer (press again for SEQ 2), 4 = mod matrix.
  • LFO knobs: shape (sine, tri, saw, square, sample + hold), rate, tempo sync, depth. LFOs restart on every note.
  • Sequencer: the main knob moves the step cursor; knobs set the step value, length (1-16), rate (synced to tempo) and glide. Hold B to clear it. Both sequencers run all the time, locked to the tempo.
  • Matrix: 8 slots. The main knob picks a slot; knobs set source, destination, amount (±100%) and VIA (a second source that scales the amount, e.g. ENV2 via LFO1). Hold B to clear a slot.
  • Sources: LFO1, LFO2, ENV1 (the note shape), ENV2, SEQ1, SEQ2, VELO, KEY. Destinations: PITCH (±1 oct), FINE (±100 cents), DETUNE, CUT1, CUT2, RES1, RES2, AMP, PAN, LFO1 RT, LFO2 RT.
  • On OSC, FILTER and MOD, Play plays a longer test note so you can hear the modulation; press twice to loop it.
  • Melody screen: pick a bar (1-4), turn the main knob to set its pitch, go to the next bar. With 4 pitches set, press F8 to find chords.
  • Suggestions blink. F8 keeps one, B rejects it, the knob scrolls through the others. Press Play to hear them.
  • After keeping chords: pick a bar (1-4) and turn the knob to move its main note; the chords re-adapt, F8 keeps the change, B undoes it. F8 with nothing pending reopens the chord suggestions.
  • Yellow = notes you placed. Cyan = notes placed by the chord finder.
  • The loop is 8 bars: your 4 chords, then the 4 again (the suggestions may change chord 8, or 7 and 8). After you keep chords, the knob moves only the main note of the selected bar; the chords re-adapt, F8 keeps the change, B undoes it.
  • On the note design screen, Play plays the note once; press Play again within 2 seconds to loop it, and once more to stop.
  • The metronome is on the Settings screen.
  • Knob 1 shares the bars between the chords (beats per chord), knob 2 picks the rhythm (each one has its own ending in bar 8), knob 3 the voicing spread.
  • Tempo, key, cutoff and resonance are on the settings screen: knob to pick, F8 to edit, knob to change, F8 when done.
