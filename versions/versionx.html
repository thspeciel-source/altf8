  • Order: SOUND (power-on lands here) > CHORDS > MELODY > TAPE. One live patch at a time; everything else is recorded on tape and costs no voices.
  • SOUND: hold button 1 + DIAL = test pitch (no key, no top or bottom; it becomes the patch's home, and chords and melody start around it), hold 2 + DIAL = what plays (note, fifth, triad, wide chord, your chords), hold 3 + DIAL = how (held, pulsing, arpeggiated). PLAY loops the test sound.
  • Sound pages: button 1 SOURCE (OSC: DIAL picks sine, tri, saw, square or none; additive, unison, sub, noise. MOD OSC: sync, ratio, ring mod, FM. FEEL: tune, level, detune, phase). Button 2 TONE (filter, filter 2, grit, space, room). Button 3 MOTION (envelope, ENV 2, LFO 1-2, SEQ 1-2, matrix, order of the effects). Button 4 PLAY IN: CHORDS or MELODY. Rates run through tempo divisions, then plain Hz with no limit. On a SEQ page DIAL moves the step cursor; on MATRIX it picks the row; on ORDER it picks the effect.
  • Modulation: on ENVELOPE, ENV 2, LFO or SEQ, tap F8 to pick up that source's cable, then turn any knob on any page: that sets how much the source moves it (left = the other way); F8 or B puts it down. Hold F8 anywhere for a quick connection: DIAL picks the source, a knob sets the depth. All of it lands in the MATRIX (sources LFO1-2, ENV1, ENV2, SEQ1-2, VELO, KEY, HIT; every knob with a value is a destination, plus ENV1 RESTART, which restarts the note envelope on each SEQ step or LFO cycle). Envelopes, velocity and key move shared things (LFO and SEQ settings, the effects) through the newest note. Settings that shape a note (envelope times, sync, additive, key track) take their modulation when each note starts.
  • CHORDS: the v48 chord finder, unchanged; the notes start around the patch's home pitch.
  • MELODY: a step sequencer. Hold a bar button to open a bar; the buttons become beats; each hit has pitch (steps through the key), velocity, length, hits per beat, nudge, chance, glide, accent, a HIT value for the matrix, and fine pitch in cents (it stops for a notch at every semitone).
  • TAPE: 8 stereo channels of the 8-bar loop. The live patch records its own sound (its effects included); the channel's EQ, volume, pan and sends stay adjustable. Bouncing channels into another records them with their reverb and delay, with only those channels playing.
  • Visualizer extension (VIEW, next to PLAY): one note of the patch as a floating 3D object, calculated from the settings (no sound needed): frequency left to right (LO to HI), the note's time front to back (START to END, echo and reverb tails at the end), and loudness as how far it swells above and below its middle, colored blue to red. The shape holds still while the sound plays and morphs when you turn a knob; while a note plays, a white slice shows where in the note you are. Each setting owns one part of the picture: FILTERS = a see-through green cap over the frequencies, shaped like the filter's response (cutoff, type, resonance bump, key track, FILTER 2 and its route), and the cutoff also sets the color range; DRIVE = jagged surface, FOLD = peaks fold back down, CRUSH = terraces, DOWNSAMPLE = wider blocks; PAN = the surface slides sideways, CHORUS = a cyan copy drifting beside it, FLANGER = comb stripes, PHASER = moving dark notches; ECHO = repeats of the note further back in time (ECHO TIME = spacing), REVERB = a smeared tail plus contour lines spreading on the floor, ROOM SIZE = floor grid and contour spread; GATE = quiet slices drop flat under a red sheet (GATE SPEED = how fast), SWELL = a rising tail plus dots climbing over the swell time; LFOs, SEQs and envelopes show up through the matrix as ripples along time when connected, ENVELOPE = the surface rises, settles and fades along time like the envelope, and its outline fades in on the far wall while you turn an envelope knob (ENV 2 in cyan), SEQs = bars on the left wall, MATRIX = a dot per connection circling the top, ORDER = the colored strip along the front edge; ADDITIVE = denser mesh, UNISON = one copy of the object per voice, side by side, DETUNE = how far apart they spread, SUB = warm floor glow at the low end, NOISE = floating dots, SYNC = saw ridges, RING MOD = checker, FM = sparkles, TUNE = shifts the colors, LEVEL = height. Any other knob sends a ripple across the surface in its color. The knob you turn is named at the top.
  • The LIMIT light next to the power light: amber when the output limiter is working lightly, red when it squashes hard.
