Switch screen (melody / note design / settings)hold Space + ←→
  • Melody screen: pick a bar (1-4), turn the main knob to set its pitch, go to the next bar. With 4 pitches set, press F8 to find chords.
  • Suggestions blink. F8 keeps one, B rejects it, the knob scrolls through the others. Press Play to hear them.
  • After confirming: pick a bar, turn the knob to a note, F8 to grab it, turn to move it, F8 to place or B to cancel. Hold B to delete.
  • Yellow = notes you placed. Cyan = notes placed by the chord finder.
  • Turning any of the 4 knobs shows the note's shape and plays it until you stop for 2 seconds.
  • On the note design screen, Play plays the note once; press Play again within 2 seconds to loop it, and once more to stop.
  • Press B (when not rejecting or cancelling) to open the selector menu at the top. Turn the knob to pick NOTES, RHYTHM, PATTERN or CHORDS, then press F8.
  • PATTERN sets how long each of the 4 chords lasts across the loop. RHYTHM sets how each chord is split into hits. All notes light up and change live as you turn the knob; F8 keeps it, B goes back to the menu.
  • CHORDS reopens the chord finder suggestions at any time.
  • Tempo, key, cutoff and resonance are on the settings screen: knob to pick, F8 to edit, knob to change, F8 when done.
