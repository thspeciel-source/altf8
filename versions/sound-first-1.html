  • Order: SOUND (power-on lands here) > CHORDS > MELODY > TAPE. One live patch at a time; everything else is recorded on tape and costs no voices.
  • SOUND: hold button 1 + DIAL = test pitch (no key, no top or bottom; it becomes the patch's home, and chords and melody start around it), hold 2 + DIAL = what plays (note, fifth, triad, wide chord, your chords), hold 3 + DIAL = how (held, pulsing, arpeggiated). PLAY loops the test sound.
  • Button 2 pages: envelope, ENV 2, LFO 1, LFO 2, SEQ 1, SEQ 2, MATRIX (16 rows). LFOs and sequencers are shared by all notes; an LFO can restart on each note. Rates run through tempo divisions, then plain Hz with no limit. On a SEQ page DIAL moves the step cursor; on MATRIX it picks the row.
  • Matrix: sources LFO1-2, ENV1 (the note envelope), ENV2, SEQ1-2, VELO, KEY, HIT (the melody's per-hit value). An LFO's rate and depth and a sequencer's rate are destinations too, so modulation can move modulation (only LFO/SEQ/HIT can move those). Amounts have no limit.
  • CHORDS: the v48 chord finder, unchanged; the notes start around the patch's home pitch.
  • MELODY: a step sequencer. Hold a bar button to open a bar; the buttons become beats; each hit has pitch (steps through the key), velocity, length, hits per beat, nudge, chance, glide, accent, a HIT value for the matrix, and fine pitch in cents (it stops for a notch at every semitone).
  • TAPE: 8 stereo channels of the 8-bar loop. The live patch records its own sound (its effects included); the channel's EQ, volume, pan and sends stay adjustable. Bouncing channels into another records them with their reverb and delay, with only those channels playing.
  • The LIMIT light next to the power light: amber when the output limiter is working lightly, red when it squashes hard.
